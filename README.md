# Fitaka

Fitaka (Financial + Pitaka) is an Android app I'm building to keep track of my money in pesos. Like Lola's pitaka with a fold for every purpose, it sorts everything into five parts: Kita (income and paydays), Baon (allowance), Ipon (savings and goals), Gastos (spending) and Utang (loans, both ways). It also shows how much sits in each wallet, such as Cash, GCash, Maya and MariBank. You can log an entry in a few taps, bring in entries from a GCash or MariBank statement, get reminders for paydays and loan due dates, and see a monthly summary with a chart. Your entries stay on your phone, with no account and no sign-up, plus an optional fingerprint lock and a backup file you keep yourself. The app goes online only to ask GitHub for a new version, and you can turn that off. It has a playful hand-drawn look with six colour themes in light and dark. This version (1.2.0) updates itself from inside the app and knows loans paid twice a month, and feedback is very welcome.
